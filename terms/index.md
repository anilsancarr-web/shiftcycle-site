## 1. What Shift Cycle is

Shift Cycle is a **personal calendar**. You describe your rotation; it projects
that pattern forward and shows you the result.

## 2. What Shift Cycle is not

**It is not your employer's roster, and it is not a record of your working
time.**

The App shows what **you** entered. It has no connection to your employer, no
access to any official rota, and no way of knowing if your shifts change. If
your workplace changes the schedule and you do not change it here, the App will
confidently show you the old one.

- Always treat your employer's own roster as the authority.
- The hours and pay figures are an **estimate** from the times you entered. They
  are not a payslip, they do not know about overtime rules, premiums, breaks,
  holiday or tax, and they should never be used to dispute pay on their own.
