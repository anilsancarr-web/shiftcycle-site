## About the app

Pick a rotation — four on four off, 2-2-3, twelve-hour days and nights, three
rotating shifts — or build your own cycle of any length for a hospital rota or an
irregular pattern. Tell it which day of the cycle you are on today and the whole
year fills in.

It works offline, stores everything on your device, and collects nothing about
you. There is no account and no server.

## Common questions

**Can I change one day without breaking the rotation?**
Yes. Tap the day, pick a different shift, add a note if you want. The rotation
carries on around it.

**My shift times are wrong.**
The presets can only guess. Settings → Shift types lets you set the real start
time and length for each one.

**The week starts on the wrong day.**
The app reads that from your phone's own calendar settings rather than assuming.
If it looks wrong, check your iOS Language & Region settings first.

**What happens after the 14-day trial?**
The app needs Pro — Lifetime or Monthly — to keep opening. **Nothing you have
entered is deleted**: your rotation and your edits come back the moment you
unlock or restore Pro.

**How do I move my rotation to a new phone?**
Settings → Back up my data, save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, then on the
new phone use Settings → Restore from a backup.

**I bought lifetime. Will that change?**
No. See the [Terms of Use](./terms/); it is written down there deliberately.
